If you're a founder or consultant recording a show, "how much does podcast editing cost?" is usually the first question. The honest answer: it depends entirely on scope — but here's a breakdown that actually helps you decide.

**The three tiers of podcast production cost:**

1. **Simple audio cleanup** — $45–$150 per episode. Cuts silence, balances levels, minimal extras.
2. **Full audio + video editing** — $150–$400 per episode. Adds multicam switching, color correction, and tighter pacing.
3. **Done-for-you repurposing** — $500–$1,500+/month. Everything above plus vertical reels, show notes, quote graphics, and social scheduling.

**What drives the price up?** Vertical reels with dynamic captions, SEO show notes, branded graphics, and scheduled distribution all add real work. A podcast that produces "one episode" that becomes "a week of content" is fundamentally a bigger job than just editing the audio.

**Does expensive mean better?** Not always. A dedicated solo producer who personally reviews each file can often deliver more consistent quality than a big agency where your episode passes through many hands. That's exactly why PlayPress Studio caps its client roster.
