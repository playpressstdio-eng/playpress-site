If you're a founder or consultant recording a show, "how much does podcast editing cost?" is usually the first question you ask. The honest answer is that it depends entirely on scope. But rather than leave it vague, here's a breakdown that actually helps you decide.

**The three tiers of podcast production cost:**

1. **Simple audio cleanup**, about $45 to $150 per episode. Cuts silence, balances levels, minimal extras. This is the "make it listenable" tier.
2. **Full audio plus video editing**, about $150 to $400 per episode. Adds multicam switching, color correction, and tighter pacing. This is where a show starts to look and feel produced.
3. **Done-for-you repurposing**, about $500 to $1,500 or more a month. Everything above, plus vertical reels, show notes, quote graphics, and social scheduling. This is where the "one episode" becomes "a week of content."

**What actually drives the price up?**

Vertical reels with dynamic captions, SEO show notes, branded graphics, and scheduled distribution all add real, countable work. A podcast that turns one recording into a full week of content isn't doing the same job as one that just cleans up the audio. The bigger job costs more, and it's usually worth it, because that's the turnaround that produces reach.

One thing worth noting: your numbers here should reflect reality, not a race to the bottom. A very cheap per-episode rate usually means someone is cutting corners or passing your show through a production line. You get what you pay for, so the honest conversation is always about what's included, not just the sticker price.

**Does expensive mean better?**

Not automatically. A dedicated solo producer who personally reviews every file can often deliver more consistent quality than a big agency where your episode passes through many hands. Because a solo producer is accountable for the whole thing, not a slice of it, the review process is tighter and your show doesn't slip between the cracks.
