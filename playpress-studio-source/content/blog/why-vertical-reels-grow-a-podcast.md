Most podcasters record a great hour-long episode and publish it, then wonder why nobody new ever discovers it. The missing piece isn't the show itself. It's the repurposing. Your episode is your library, but a vertical reel is your front door, and right now most shows don't have a front door at all.

**Why vertical short-form works for podcasts:**

- Shorts, Reels, and TikTok are where discovery actually happens. A thirty-second clip can reach people who would never scroll through a sixty-minute podcast feed.
- The platform algorithm pushes vertical video hard, so a strong hook clip can outperform your entire back catalog in terms of single-video reach.
- It's essentially free distribution. One episode can become three to five clips that keep working for you all week, and you only had to record once.

**The parts that matter in a clip:**

1. **A hook in the first two seconds.** This decides whether anyone keeps watching. If the first moment doesn't grab them, nothing after it gets a chance.
2. **Dynamic captions.** Most people watch with the sound off. Captions are your audio made visible, and they're what keeps a silent scroller watching.
3. **Face-framing.** Keep the speaker in frame even when they move, so the edit feels intentional rather than jumpy.
4. **Dead air removed.** A vertical reel has no patience. Every second should move, and any pause that lingers loses the viewer.

**The trap to avoid:**

The most common mistake is cutting clips that just repeat the episode. A clip that's basically "here's the whole conversation, condensed" doesn't earn attention, it just recaps something nobody saw. The best short-form pulls a single sharp, standalone idea: one quote, one contrarian take, one short story, and presents it cleanly on its own. That's a different skill from editing an episode, and it's exactly what a done-for-you producer handles so you don't have to learn it from scratch.

**What this means for your show:**

If your show isn't generating short-form yet, that's usually the single biggest missed opportunity you have. It's also one of the fastest things to fix, because you already have the raw material, you just need someone to find the moments worth clipping.
