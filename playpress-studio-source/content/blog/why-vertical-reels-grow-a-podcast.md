Most podcasters record a great hour-long episode and publish it — then wonder why nobody new discovers it. The missing piece isn't the show; it's the **repurposing**.

**Why vertical short-form works for podcasts:**
- Shorts, Reels, and TikTok are where discovery happens. A 30-second clip can reach people who'd never scroll a 60-minute podcast feed.
- The platform algorithm pushes vertical video hard, so a strong hook clip can outperform your entire back catalog in reach.
- It's free distribution. One episode can become 3–5 clips that keep working all week.

**The parts that matter in a clip:**
1. **A hook in the first 2 seconds.** This decides whether anyone keeps watching.
2. **Dynamic captions.** Most people watch silently — captions are your audio made visible.
3. **Face-framing.** Keep the speaker in frame even when they move, so the edit feels intentional.
4. **Dead air removed.** A vertical reel has no patience; every second should move.

**The trap to avoid:** cutting clips that just repeat the episode. The best short-form pulls a single sharp, standalone idea — one quote, one contrarian take, one story — and presents it clean. That's a skill, and it's exactly what a done-for-you producer handles so you don't have to.

If your show isn't generating short-form yet, that's usually the single biggest missed opportunity. It's also the fastest thing to fix.
