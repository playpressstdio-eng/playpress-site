The Process

Simple, predictable, zero back-and-forth

A streamlined 3-step workflow designed so editing never takes you away from running your business.

Step 01

1. Drop the raw file

Upload your audio or video recording and guest info to your dedicated Drive folder. Your production work ends right there.

Step 02

2. Production & QC

I balance and master the audio (-16 LUFS), cut out dead air, pull vertical reels, draft copy, and personally review every file before you see it.

Step 03

3. Approved & published

Once you give the green light, I schedule the full episode to your podcast host and YouTube, then roll out your clips across social media all week.

About the Producer

Agency-level output, delivered entirely solo

Hi, I'm John Lloyd Sarez — founder and only producer of PlayPress Studio. I help founders and consultants turn every recorded episode into a full week of content, without them lifting a finger past the record button.

My path in wasn't the usual one. I graduated in Computer Engineering, and the pandemic hit before I'd ever set foot in a corporate office. My first break was writing podcast show-notes — back when that meant actually listening to every episode and writing it out by hand. One task led to the next: editing video, running socials, then leading a team of virtual assistants across a slate of client shows for over four years.

That time taught me the difference between content that's finished and content that's done right. When a show moves through many hands, an episode rarely gets one person's full, honest attention. So I built PlayPress Studio to be the opposite: one producer, your show end-to-end, and a roster I keep small on purpose.

I keep it to three clients at a time because it's the only way I can honestly promise the polish and the direct 1-on-1 access that scale simply can't.

Off the clock: I read a lot of Chinese web novels — xianxia and xuanhuan, the long cultivation epics. Think Wattpad, but the authors are Chinese. And when it comes to choosing a hook or a cut, I go completely on gut feel. It hasn't steered me wrong yet.

Just your raw audio/video files, your guest's name/bio, and any links you referenced. I handle the entire pipeline after recording.

How fast is the turnaround?

Standard delivery is 48 to 72 hours from receiving complete raw files.

How do revisions work?

Every episode includes up to two rounds of minor revisions requested within 5 business days of delivery.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Retainers are billed monthly with a simple 14-day written notice if you ever need to pause or cancel.
